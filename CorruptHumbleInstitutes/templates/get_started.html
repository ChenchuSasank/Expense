💰 Welcome to Expense Tracker

📱 What is this App?
This is your personal finance manager that helps you track every rupee you earn and spend. Monitor your daily expenses, categorize your transactions, and get insights into your spending habits.

🔐 Your Data is Private & Secure

✅ Device-Based Storage: Your data is stored privately on this device/browser

✅ No Data Sharing: Only you can see your financial records

✅ User Isolation: Each user has completely separate data storage

✅ Secure Access: Data is protected by user authentication or device fingerprinting

🎯 How to Use This App:

1️⃣ Record Data: Add your income and expenses daily

2️⃣ Show Data: View all your financial transactions

3️⃣ Modify Data: Edit or delete existing records

4️⃣ Reports: See visual charts of your spending patterns

5️⃣ Categories: Organize transactions by type (Food, Rent, Salary, etc.)
